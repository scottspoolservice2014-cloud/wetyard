Catch Basins for Yard Drainage

Catch basins collect surface water at low points and route it into a drainage line. They can fit some pooling problems when there is a suitable place to discharge the water.

What this type of work does

Catch basins collect surface water at low points and route it into a drainage line. They can fit some pooling problems when there is a suitable place to discharge the water.

When a professional may evaluate it

This type of work may be considered when the property’s symptoms and water path line up with what the system is designed to handle. A provider may need to evaluate elevations, soil, existing drains, discharge options, utilities and local rules before recommending a design.

Before hiring

Ask what problem the proposed work is intended to solve, where the collected water will go, what happens during heavy rainfall, and whether permits, utility locating or additional site work may be needed.
