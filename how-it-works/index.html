How WetYard Works

Describe the problem, understand the likely service category, browse local providers or ask WetYard to help narrow the match.

1. Start with the symptom

Tell us what the water is doing, when it appears and where it collects. WetYard uses that information to narrow broad service categories—not to make a professional diagnosis.

2. See relevant local providers

Provider listings are tagged by the services and areas they publicly advertise. You can use the directory without giving WetYard your contact information.

3. Ask WetYard to help

If you submit a help request, WetYard may use the details you provide to identify and connect you with a local service provider that appears relevant to the request.

4. You choose what happens next

The contractor handles estimates, scheduling, pricing, contracts and the work itself. WetYard does not guarantee that a provider will accept a request or that a particular solution will be appropriate.
