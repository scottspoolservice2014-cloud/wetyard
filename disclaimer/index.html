Public drainage and property boundaries

Drainage responsibilities can depend on property lines, easements, rights-of-way, municipal systems, county systems, homeowner associations and other factors. Confirm responsibility before authorizing private work on a feature that may be publicly or jointly maintained.

Emergency conditions

If water is entering a structure, creating an electrical hazard, undermining a structure or presenting another immediate safety risk, contact the appropriate emergency or qualified local professional rather than relying on an online guide.
