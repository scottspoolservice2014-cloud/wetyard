Water Pooling Near Your House

Water collecting near a foundation deserves attention. Grading, downspouts and surface drainage are common categories a professional may evaluate.

Start with what the water is doing

The visible symptom does not always identify the cause. A local professional may evaluate grade, soil, runoff paths, downspouts, irrigation and existing drainage before recommending work.

Not sure who to call? WetYard can help narrow the type of local professional that may fit your property problem.

Common next steps

Depending on the property, professionals may evaluate grading, catch basins, surface drains, French drains, swales, culverts, downspout routing or irrigation-related causes. Avoid choosing a solution solely from an online symptom.
