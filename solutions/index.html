Understand the options

These are categories a professional may evaluate after identifying the source and path of the water.

French drains

Subsurface collection and conveyance.

Grading

Reshaping slope so water moves appropriately.

Catch basins

Surface inlets for low spots.

Channel drains

Linear drainage for driveways and hardscapes.

Dry wells

Temporary storage and soil infiltration where suitable.

Swales and culverts

Move runoff through yards and crossings.

Downspout drainage

Route roof water away from problem areas.

Surface drainage

Collect water that stays above ground.

Subsurface drainage

Manage water moving through saturated soil.
