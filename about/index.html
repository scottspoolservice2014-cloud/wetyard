WetYard

Why WetYard Exists

WetYard is an independent problem-first directory built to help homeowners understand yard-water problems and find local professionals whose published services may fit the work.

What WetYard does

WetYard starts with the homeowner’s symptom—standing water, flooding after rain, soggy areas, runoff, erosion or another outdoor water problem—rather than assuming a specific fix. We organize local providers by the work they publicly say they perform, then give homeowners two choices: browse providers themselves or ask WetYard to help narrow the fit.

What WetYard does not do

WetYard is not a drainage contractor, engineer, plumber, landscaper or government agency. We do not diagnose properties, guarantee provider availability, set contractor pricing or guarantee workmanship. A professional on site may reach a different conclusion than an online problem description suggests.

How listings work

Basic directory listings are assembled from publicly available business and service information. A listing does not mean the business has paid WetYard, partnered with WetYard or endorsed WetYard. We aim to keep service and coverage details useful, but homeowners should confirm current licensing, insurance, availability and scope directly with the provider where applicable.

Why the directory is visible

You do not have to submit a lead to use WetYard. Browse the directory and contact a provider yourself if you prefer. If you want help narrowing the options, use the Problem Finder and request a match.
